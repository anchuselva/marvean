Instead of blindly compressing all tensor columns, the engine profiles actual activation variances: the most sensitive 1% of tensor channels remain in high-precision FP16/FP8 while the bulk 99% are quantized into dense INT4 kernels. This guarantees that complex reasoning benchmarks (GSM8K, MATH, and MMLU) suffer less than +0.02 perplexity drift while saving 73% of your cluster's VRAM.

Simple queries are handled directly by agile 3B–8B parameter SLMs, while high-entropy or multi-step logic challenges are dynamically routed to 70B+ frontier models.
